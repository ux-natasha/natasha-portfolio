## Lede

Two travelers who wanted the same thing for different reasons: one priced
out of solo travel, one with the budget but no one to go with.

## Body

WanderLink matches travelers with locals and other travelers by shared
interests, location, and travel dates. I built it around two personas drawn
from the research: an extroverted twenty-something priced out of solo
travel, and an introverted engineer in her thirties with the budget but no
one to go with. I ran the competitive audit myself, mapping WanderLink
against Booking, TripAdvisor, Hotels.com, MakeMyTrip, and Trip.com on the
features that actually mattered — messaging, verification, interest-based
search — rather than the ones every travel app already has. The visual
system runs on teal against orange and yellow accents, with Wix Made For
carrying the UI text and Agrandir reserved for headings.
