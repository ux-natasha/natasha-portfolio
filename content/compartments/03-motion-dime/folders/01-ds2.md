## Lede

The feature store's own materials couldn't explain what it did. I rebuilt
the site, drew the explainer video, and prototyped it to see if any of that
actually landed.

## Body

DS2 sold a feature store, and their existing materials didn't communicate
what one was or why it mattered. I redesigned the site around a set of
original illustrations built to carry the technical concept without
flattening it, then worked with an animator to turn the same visual language
into an explainer video — his motion, my illustrations. I prototyped the
site separately, testing it with users and iterating on what confused people
versus what didn't need to be there. The harder part wasn't drawing; it was
deciding, screen by screen, how much of the underlying technical process a
feature-store buyer actually needed to see.
