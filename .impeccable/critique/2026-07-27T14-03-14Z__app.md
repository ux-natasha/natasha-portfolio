## Overall Impression

One genuinely beautiful object set in the house style of the last three thousand portfolios. The value-stepped folder and the prose are the assets. The biggest problem is not aesthetic: the reward gradient runs backwards. The thesis is "scroll deeper, get more"; levels 1-3 deliver, level 4 in all four chapters is stubs stamped `needs your dump` with bodies written in the second person to Natasha, level 5 is a locked door announcing a stand-in password onto "Nothing is here yet."

## What's Working

1. **The folder's value-stepped construction** — four real tone steps (OKLCH L 0.958 -> 0.905 -> 0.877 -> 0.841) plus one tight contact shadow; the -1px -7px -8px -1px cover inset protrudes on two sides only. Depth via value separation is resolution-independent, survives print and forced-colours, and never gets the floating-sticker problem blur-depth has.
2. **components/Figures.tsx** — one 560-unit measure across all four drawings so stroke weight and label size are shared; one pink mark per figure spent on the single fact the drawing exists to make; DelayPath rations black to the deepest level because that is what caused the slip. Descriptive `<title>` elements are the accessibility high point.
3. **The N>=2 shelf gate** — shelfIsReal() (schema.ts:132) + firstWrittenChapter()/getWrittenChapters() (content.ts:164-176) make "structure cannot outrun substance" a property of the data model rather than a discipline.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Every conversion path terminates at a fake email address
**What:** content/landing.md:60-64 sets contact to hello@example.com with placeholder:true, rendering "placeholder — the public address goes here" in pink (Colophon.tsx:25-29). Same href is the target of "Ask for one" on /enter, "say which screen" on /screens, and every level-5 gate in all four chapters. Verified in source.
**Why:** a portfolio has one job beyond impressing — being contactable. There is no working way to reach Natasha from any surface, and the site says so in the accent colour.
**Fix:** real address, or drop the mailto and point at a working LinkedIn/GitHub URL.
**Command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P0] Level 4 renders eleven production notes addressed to the site owner
**What:** Chapter.tsx:257 renders `needs your dump` for every [stub] heading — 2 in ch.00, 3 each in 01/02/03 = 11 instances in --pink-ink. Bodies read "The guardrails you designed for that go here." Verified in source.
**Why:** level 4 is the payoff of the central mechanic; three clicks of the rewarded behaviour returns the author's to-do list. Violates §1's "mark once, quietly, per block".
**Fix:** filter [stub] details in splitDetails() (content.ts:228-242); if a level has no non-stub content, do not offer the level. Stub prompts belong in markdown as comments.
**Command:** /impeccable harden

### [P1] Twelve font sizes between 8px and 13px; no adjacent step reaches 1.25
**What:** 8, 8.48, 9, 9.44, 10, 10.4, 10.56, 11, 11.36, 12, 12.48, 13px, plus 9px/10px hard-coded in SVGs. Adjacent ratios 1.015-1.061; the 1.015 pair (0.65rem vs 0.66rem) is 0.16px apart as two separate tokens. 41 declarations across 24 selectors sit below the 12px floor.
**Why:** one typographic tier split twelve ways. Hierarchy is carried by two bits (uppercase-or-not, one of three colours) across ~25 roles, so the composition reads as texture rather than structure. 8px uppercase letterspaced text (.rail__up, .rail__pos, .pictorial) is functionally illegible.
**Fix:** collapse to three small sizes (0.6875 / 0.875 / 1rem) and three uppercase roles max; delete 0.5, 0.53, 0.59, 0.65, 0.66, 0.71, 0.78rem; raise .fig__label to 11px.
**Command:** /impeccable typeset

### [P1] Depth is one-way, unnamed, and destroyed on every chapter switch
**What:** Chapter.tsx:169 renders one button that only increments; no collapse control exists in the codebase. Slice.tsx:107 calls setDepth(0) inside land(). Button reads `+4 more levels` and never names what is inside. .rail__depth dots are aria-hidden spans, not controls. All four chapters at full depth = 16 unlabelled irreversible clicks. Verified in source.
**Why:** turns the best idea into a slot machine. Readers cannot know three clicks yields an original diagram, so most never find one — which is why first-contact imagery is effectively zero.
**Fix:** name the next level on the button (labels exist in frontmatter); make .rail__dot real buttons; add a collapse; preserve depth across chapter switches.
**Command:** /impeccable layout

### [P1] Mobile fails 44x44 structurally, and mobile sees zero colour
**What:** at <=54rem, .rail__tab renders ~38x30px; .rail__up (the only route to the drawer) is ~58x22px; the rail is height:46px so no control inside it can ever reach 44px; .folder__tab ~36px; the primary CTA .descend has a 10px label. Every pink reveal (.descend__track::after, .depth-open__dot, .chapter__next-title::after, .colophon__address-text::after, .plate__link) is :hover-gated, so touch visitors see a fully monochrome site.
**Why:** recruiters open portfolio links on phones.
**Fix:** raise the mobile rail to ~52px and the tabs with it; give .descend a real hit area; add @media (hover: none) states so at least one pink mark is present at rest.
**Command:** /impeccable adapt

### [P2] Two contrast failures, in a system with no headroom
**What:** .spine[data-soon="true"] — --gray #696961 on rgb(207 203 187 / .16) over --rail-bg = effective #e7e4d9 -> 4.35:1 at 11px, FAILS AA. This element's contrast was already fixed once from 2.06:1; the fix introduced the wash and nobody re-measured. Separately --pink #d4537e as focus ring = 2.42:1 on --cover, fails WCAG 1.4.11 (3:1) on .folder__tab and .spine.
**Systemic:** every warm-neutral pair lands between 4.56 and 5.16 — the palette is tuned 1-15% above the legal minimum at 8-12px, so any tone tweak breaks it.
**Fix:** --gray-on-cover on the soon-spine (6.4:1) or drop the wash; darken the focus ring toward --pink-ink or add a 1px dark inner ring.
**Command:** /impeccable audit

### [P2] The folder tab row overflows the page below ~356px
**What:** .folder__tab white-space:nowrap inside .folder__tabs, a plain flex with no overflow-x. Chapter 00 active measures ~308px against 312px available at 360px viewport — 4px clearance. At 320px it overflows ~40px and the page scrolls sideways. .shelf__spines got overflow-x:auto at <=46rem; the primary nav control did not.
**Fix:** same overflow-x:auto; scrollbar-width:none below 46rem, or drop the title from the active tab at mobile.
**Command:** /impeccable adapt

### [P3] The drawer shipped with one dead label and no 404
**What:** content/projects/writing/folder.md exists with status:coming; shelfIsReal() counts it, so the shelf renders twice and ch.03 hands off to a drawer containing the folder you are in plus a dashed box that 404s. No app/not-found.tsx or error.tsx exists (verified), so /writing hits Next's unstyled default. CLAUDE.md §12 reserved this decision for Natasha.
**Fix:** delete content/projects/writing/, or make shelfIsReal() count only status:"open". Add not-found.tsx either way.
**Command:** /impeccable harden

## Questions to Consider

1. The spec spent 38,000 words preventing this site from reading as a document, and it now has a hairline above and below .chapter__meta, a rule under every .breakdown__row and .slots__row, a rule at .chapter__end, and a trailing rule after every level eyebrow. Could Natasha tell this build apart from the "attempt 1" version she rejected as a resume, or has the failure just moved from the meta block into the breakdown?
2. Pink is absent at rest and touch devices have no hover. What colour does the mobile half of the audience ever see — and if the answer is none, is the 2%-pink rule a design principle or a design that only exists in one browser?
3. Levels 4 and 5 — the depth the whole thesis rests on — are unwritten and locked in all four chapters. Is this a monograph or a table of contents for one? If a plain grid with four real case studies would show a hiring manager more actual work, what is the folder buying?
4. Which does more damage to a hiring decision: showing no work, or showing eleven notes to yourself reading `needs your dump`?
5. Fraunces, Inter, and a cream at OKLCH L 0.95 / C 0.010 / H 93.6 are all dead-centre defaults. The value-stepped folder is the only thing here nobody else has. Why is it 820px wide and half-hidden below the fold instead of being the entire first screen?
